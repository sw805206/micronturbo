Light to carry.
Long on power.

Anything working away from a socket carries its own power — a rescue truck, an industrial drone, a walking robot — and pays for it in weight, in service hours, in fuel it cannot find. Lithium and piston have each pushed one of those limits and surrendered another. Micronturbo moves every one of them.

01

What a micro gas turbine changes

Mobility

A generator that cannot reach the site may as well not exist. The jobs that need power most — a collapsed building, a ridge line, a fourth floor with no lift — are the ones a wheeled box never reaches.

9–12 kg for 6 kW
Lithium station45 kg for 3.6 kW, about one hour per charge
Piston set50–120 kg for 6 kW
Efficiency

Power per kilogram decides whether a drone flies for thirty minutes or five hours, and whether a robot works a shift or an errand. It is the number that has held this category still.

up to 2,000 W/kg
Lithium stationAbout 80 W/kg at pack level
Piston set50–130 W/kg, depending on the model
Toughness

A machine that will not turn over at −40 °C is not underperforming, it is absent — and so is a battery that gave up its charge overnight. Altitude does the same more politely, taking half your output on the way up.

−40 °C, 75% at 4000 m
Lithium station20–40% of capacity at −20 °C; cold charging damages it
Piston setNo cold start at −40 °C; 50% power at 4000 m
Versatility

The fuel on site is whatever the supply chain delivered, not what the manual asked for. Burning eight of them means the machine adapts to the place instead of the place adapting to the machine.

8 fuels, AC + DC, refuel in minutes
Lithium stationRefills from grid or solar, hours not minutes
Piston setOne fuel, AC output only
Durability

A generator’s real price is what it costs across every hour you run it, not what you paid at the counter. A machine that fades as it ages charges you twice — and ours is still at rated output in hour 2,900.

3000+ hours
Lithium station~3,500 cycles to 80% of original capacity
Piston set500–800 hours before replacement
Sustainability

Point-of-use accounting flatters anything with a plug, but the carbon does not disappear — it moves upstream to whatever filled the battery. A fuel you choose is a carbon story you control.

Hydrogen + methanol
Lithium stationDepends on the upstream grid
