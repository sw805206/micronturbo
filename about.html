The Engineering Behind the Numbers.

Every figure on this site — 3,000 hours, −40 °C, eight fuels — comes from one program, built by one team, on its own intellectual property. This page is who that team is and how far the hardware has actually run.

Micronturbo was founded out of the turbomachinery program at Beihang University, under an academician of the Chinese Academy of Engineering. The architecture is forward-engineered rather than adapted from an existing engine, and the intellectual property is wholly the company's own.

The Number the Next Industries Run On

A rescue team, a polar station and an air taxi have one thing that defines their power: watts per kilogram. It took the careers above to raise that number to a new high.

The compute ceiling

A walking robot's onboard AI asks for more power than a pack its own size can give it. Power density is what decides whether the machine works a shift or runs an errand.

Endurance anxiety

An aircraft that has to land and charge is an aircraft with routes it cannot fly. Range and flight time are set by the weight of the energy on board, not by the airframe.

Effective payload

Every kilogram of generator carried into a disaster site is a kilogram of rescue equipment that stayed behind. What the crew can bring is what the power source leaves them.
