The Engineering Behind the Numbers.

Every figure on this site — 3,000 hours, −40 °C, eight fuels — comes from one program, built by one team, on its own intellectual property. This page introduces the team, and the work.

Micronturbo began in the turbomachinery research group at Beihang University, led by an academician of the Chinese Academy of Engineering, and built over years of sustained work by that team. One hundred percent forward-engineered, one hundred percent its own intellectual property. After a decade in research, the program has now formally entered engineering, commercialization and international rollout.

Every Industry That Moves Depends on the Same Number

A rescue team, a polar station and an air taxi all have one thing setting their power ceiling: power density. This team spent their careers raising that number to a new high.

Raising the compute ceiling

A walking robot's processors draw power on the same order as its movement does. Power density decides not just how strong the machine is, but how smart it can afford to be.

Solving endurance anxiety

An aircraft that keeps having to land and charge is an aircraft with routes it cannot finish. A light, compact power source gives every kilogram back to payload and distance.

Increasing effective payload

Every kilogram carried into a disaster zone or a polar station should go to the people who need it, not to the generator. The smaller and lighter the power source, the higher the success rate.
