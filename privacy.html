What We Collect

When you submit the contact form, we receive:

Why We Collect It

To read your message and reply to it. We use your email address to respond to your inquiry and to any follow-up conversation that grows out of it. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not send marketing email.

Where It Goes

Contact form submissions are stored in Google Sheets, part of Google Workspace, on Google's servers. Google processes this information on our behalf as our service provider. Depending on where you are, this means your information is transferred to and stored in a country other than your own, including the United States.

We do not sell your information, rent it, or share it with anyone for their own purposes.

Cookies and Tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. There is no advertising on it, and no third-party tracking scripts.

We load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when a page loads. Google receives your IP address as part of that request. This is the only third-party request the site makes.

How Long We Keep It

We keep contact form submissions for as long as we may reasonably need them for the inquiry and any business relationship that follows, and then we delete them. Where the law requires us to keep records for longer, we do.

Your Choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Use the contact form to make the request, and tell us plainly what you want done.

We do not operate an email address for these requests, so the contact form is the route. If your request is that we delete everything, we will delete the form submissions we hold and confirm when it is done.
