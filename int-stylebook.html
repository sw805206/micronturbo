Stylebook

Every token and pattern in STYLE.css, rendered live. If something here looks wrong, STYLE.css is wrong. The decisions behind these values are recorded in STYLE.md; this page is the proof they work.

This page ships in the same commit as STYLE.css, always. A stylebook that lags the CSS reports a state the site is not in.

Text on dark

--mt-text · #F6F3EE · ~15:1 · body and headings

--mt-text-2 · #C6CDD6 · ~10:1 · secondary copy

--mt-text-3 · #8A93A0 · ~5.3:1 · muted copy and captions

The line above in --mt-text-faint is shown at body size deliberately, so the failure is visible rather than theoretical. It is legal for rules, dividers and ornament. It is not legal for text a reader needs.

Ignition and amber are substituted for their deep variants inside this wrapper. Neither original is legal as text on a light ground.
