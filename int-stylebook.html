This page ships in the same commit as STYLE.css, always. A stylebook that lags the CSS reports a state the site is not in.

Categorical order

The fixed assignment order for anything counted rather than ranked.

c1 ignition c2 amber c3 steel c4 hydro c5 magnet c6 vortex

c1 and c2 are 23° apart in hue and must NOT sit adjacent as chart series — side by side they read as one colour in two lightnesses, and the distinction disappears entirely for a red-green colour-blind reader. A two-series chart takes c1 and c3. The six are safe where they are spatially separated and individually labelled, as in the point card below.

Product Page Patterns

STYLE.css section 10. Each carries its own breakpoints rather than the global 768 — these are content-shaped grids and they break where their content breaks. Resize the window to see them go.

Brand callout · .mt-callout

The box spans its container and the text fills the box. The inset comes from the padding alone — there is deliberately no max-width on the paragraph, because capping the text inside a full-width box leaves dead space down the right and makes the box look broken rather than measured.

Single-sided ignition border, so no radius — the data column idiom. On the hub it replaces the section heading rather than sitting under one.

Full-measure lead · .mt-lead--full

.mt-lead — capped at 62ch. Right for sustained reading, and this line stops well short of the container edge to show where the cap falls.

.mt-lead mt-lead--full — the cap released. A section lead is a line or two introducing the thing beneath it, so it runs the full width of the section rather than stopping around 800px above a grid that reaches 1190.

The rule: a paragraph in a full-width section runs the full width; a paragraph in a grid column takes its column. Not applied to the SKU hero intros, which are already measured by their column.

Flipped
--flip puts the media second

--flip swaps the sides with order, not with source order. The media stays first in the DOM, so below 860px every row stacks image-then-text and the flip resets rather than alternating.

The scrim is a --mt-bg gradient built from --mt-bg-rgb at 0.15 to 0.5, over an image held at 70% opacity on a --mt-bg ground. Shown here with no image behind it, so the scrim itself is visible; on the hub it is what keeps the word legible over an arbitrary photograph. The word sits bottom-left, in the dense end of the gradient and clear of whatever the picture is of.
