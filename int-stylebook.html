Categorical order

The fixed assignment order for anything counted rather than ranked.

c1 ignition c2 amber c3 steel c4 hydro c5 magnet c6 vortex

c1 and c2 are 23° apart in hue and must NOT sit adjacent as chart series — side by side they read as one color in two lightnesses, and the distinction disappears entirely for a red-green color-blind reader. A two-series chart takes c1 and c3. The six are safe where they are spatially separated and individually labeled, as in the point card below.

Product Page Patterns

STYLE.css section 10. Each carries its own breakpoints rather than the global 768 — these are content-shaped grids and they break where their content breaks. Resize the window to see them go.

Brand callout · .mt-callout

The box spans its container and the text fills the box. The inset comes from the padding alone — there is deliberately no max-width on the paragraph, because capping the text inside a full-width box leaves dead space down the right and makes the box look broken rather than measured.

Single-sided ignition border, so no radius — the data column idiom. On the hub it replaces the section heading rather than sitting under one.

Full-measure lead · .mt-lead--full

.mt-lead — capped at 62ch. Right for sustained reading, and this line stops well short of the container edge to show where the cap falls.

.mt-lead mt-lead--full — the cap released. A section lead is a line or two introducing the thing beneath it, so it runs the full width of the section rather than stopping around 800px above a grid that reaches 1190.

The rule: a paragraph in a full-width section runs the full width; a paragraph in a grid column takes its column. Not applied to the SKU hero intros, which are already measured by their column.

Form Patterns

The first components here that take input rather than present it. Every state is rendered: resting, focused, error, and both outcomes of the status block. STYLE.css section 11; the decisions are in STYLE.md section 6.

Semantic color

Error
--mt-error · #FF4F79
~5.5:1 · message text and icon only

Semantic, not categorical. It takes no slot in the c1–c6 order and nothing counted may reference it. It is never a field border, outline or fill — the submit button is the only ignition-bordered box on a page, and an outlined error field would compete with it for exactly the attention the button needs. There is deliberately no --mt-inv-error: no light-ground page exists, and inverted variants are not written speculatively.

Resting

.mt-form caps at --mt-form-width. .mt-form__row is two columns collapsing at 560px — read off the content, not the page gutter: a name field needs about 264px to show a full name, and two of those with a --mt-space-5 gap is 552px. Narrow the window past 560 and the row above should become one column.

Focused

Ignition border plus a soft ring at --mt-space-1, built from --mt-ignition-rgb at 0.28 alpha. A ring rather than a thicker border: a border that changes width reflows the field by a pixel and the row twitches on tab. Shown here through .mt-field--focus, a forced-state hook that shares the real :focus-visible declaration block rather than copying it — tab into any field above to confirm the live state matches. Transition honors prefers-reduced-motion.

Error

That does not look like a complete email address.

The field border is UNCHANGED — this is the point of the pattern. The message carries --mt-error and the vendored circle-alert icon takes currentColor from it, so color is never the only channel: the icon and the words say the same thing. Icon is Lucide, ISC, recorded in SCOPE.md. aria-invalid and aria-describedby are markup, not styling, and belong on every real instance.

Consent line

We use what you send here to reply to you, and for nothing else.

Send

Beside the submit rather than above it. The consent line is the last thing read before the action, which is where a consent statement belongs. It wraps below the button on a narrow column rather than compressing it.

Status block

Thank you.

Your message is with us. Somebody will reply within two working days.

That did not send.

Something went wrong on our end. Try again, or write to us directly.

Success reuses --mt-hydro. No success token was added: hydro already means the good outcome everywhere else on the site, and a second green would be a distinction nobody could name. Single-sided border and therefore no radius, following the data-column idiom in section 06.

Honeypot

A .mt-form__trap field is rendered directly above this line and should be invisible. If you can see an input here, the rule is not loading.

NOT .mt-visually-hidden, which is the reuse section 8 would lead you to expect. That utility hides something from the eye while keeping it in the accessibility tree — the opposite of what a honeypot needs, since a screen-reader user would find the field, fill it in, and be classified as a bot for using assistive technology. The markup half is not optional: aria-hidden="true", tabindex="-1" and autocomplete="off" on every instance.

Privacy Policy

Effective August 15, 2026

The measure is the whole pattern. This paragraph and the ones below it are capped at 62ch, and the specimen is only useful at a wide viewport — widen the window until the container stops growing and the text does not.

What We Collect

An h2 inside .mt-doc takes space above it and drops to --mt-text-h3. The element is still an h2; only its size changed. Compare it with the section headings above, which are the same element at full --mt-text-h2 size, and the difference is the point — those separate whole sections, this separates two paragraphs.

  • Base list styling is inherited, not overridden
  • Lists take the measure from the container like everything else
  • Nothing here is specific to lists

Where It Goes

A second heading, to show the rhythm repeating. A legal page carries roughly fifteen of these, which is the case the size drop answers.

简体中文的行长

这一段与上方英文段落使用同一条规则，宽度却不同。中文页面的根元素带有 zh-Hans，因此 .mt-doc 本身改用中文字体，ch 随之按中文字体的字符宽度计算， 上限收窄。两种语言各自得到符合本语言的行长，而声明只有一条。

The two blocks above must NOT be the same width. English computes 636px, Chinese 551px, from the one max-width: 62ch. ch is a unit of the script being set, so the same declaration lands near 62 Latin characters and near 34 Han characters — each inside its own comfortable measure, which one pixel value could not do for both. Equal widths here mean :lang(zh) is not reaching .mt-doc, and the Chinese page is being measured in a font it does not use.

The Chinese block is a MEASURE specimen, not a language specimen. It says only what it demonstrates — do not read it as translated copy.

Surface ground, 3px ignition top border, no radius — the .mt-stat idiom from section 5. Name at --mt-text-lg/700, the Han name muted beneath it, the role in amber at --mt-text-xs tracked 0.16em and uppercased, then an unmarkered list of credentials behind a 1px rule.

The Han name takes lang="zh-Hans" on its own element. Without it the glyphs fall to whatever the Latin stack resolves them to on an English page, since the --mt-font-cjk switch in section 7 keys off the lang attribute and nothing else. The role is amber rather than ignition: ignition is reserved for the section rule and the page's primary action, and the role is the one thing the eye scans for across four cards.
